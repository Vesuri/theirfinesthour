Version 2.0b (13.10.2026) by Vesuri (beta):
 - Enhanced framerate: faster, tear-free rendering and full ground detail on
   fast Amigas, with consistent game speed at different frame rates
 - Corrected controls, aiming, bullet hits, bombing, AI and gameplay timing
   with Enhanced framerate enabled
 - Adjustable mouse sensitivity: 1x, 2x (default), 4x or 8x
 - Improved flight recording and playback, with 128-second recordings
 - Fixed repeating explosion and splash sounds
 - Enhanced framerate is enabled in the supplied game icons (CUSTOM2=1).
   Uncheck it in the WHDLoad startup options to use the original game mode
 - New enhanced movie saves require Enhanced framerate in this slave;
   original movie files can still be loaded
 - Requires WHDLoad 17+, 512 KB ChipMem and 768 KB OtherMem
